The AFTR standard

Every piece goes through four steps.

STEP 1

Sourced

Picked one by one for brand, condition and style. No bulk junk.

STEP 2

Checked

Labels, stitching and tags inspected. If it isn’t 100% authentic, it isn’t listed.

STEP 3

Cleaned & graded

Washed or steamed, measured, and every flaw photographed. No surprises.

STEP 4

Shipped

Packed with care and sent within 48 hours from Alicante.

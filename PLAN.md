# Lote: German for a job in Germany

**One line:** a daily, 3–4 hour German program, with an AI tutor plus live small-group classes, for people who want a job in Germany. €149/month. The goal is passing the B1/B2 exam and getting hired.

**Target:** about €60,000 profit in 8 months. The "target" scenario below reaches about €70k. The slow scenario reaches about €44k.

---

## Why this idea

You asked for something people spend 3–4 hours a day on and pay €150–200 a month for with a smile. Few things meet both conditions. This one does:

| Requirement | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| 3–4 hours a day | Getting from zero to B1/B2 takes roughly 600–900 hours of study. Serious learners already do 3–4 hours a day. |
| Pays €149/month gladly | The payoff is a job in Germany, often €2,500–3,500/month gross. A €149 course that gets you there is cheap. |
| Large, growing demand | Germany has a severe shortage of nurses, care workers, electricians, drivers, IT staff and others. The Skilled Immigration Act (2023/24) made entry easier, but German at B1/B2 is still the gate. |
| A solo founder can build it | AI handles most of the practice hours. Freelance teachers handle live classes. No big investment is needed. |
| Customers who also pay for others | Recruitment agencies and employers recruiting foreign nurses and workers already pay for language training. One deal can mean 20–50 seats. |

**Why not a cheap consumer app?** To reach €60k profit with a €15 app you'd need thousands of users in a few months. That takes luck or a big ad budget. With €149 you need about 340 active members by month 8, a number you can reach through direct sales and communities.

---

## The product

**Daily structure (3–4 hours):**
1. **Live group class, 60 min.** Max 15 people, a real teacher, grouped by level (A1 → B2).
2. **AI speaking partner, 45–60 min.** Voice conversation practice with corrections, set in real job scenarios: hospital shift handover, job interview, calling a landlord.
3. **Guided practice, 60–90 min.** Vocabulary with spaced repetition, listening, reading and writing tasks marked by AI.
4. **Exam mode (weekly).** Full mock exams in the Goethe/telc format with scores.

**What makes people stay:**
- A visible progress bar toward the exam date: "You are 62% of the way to B1."
- A streak and study-hours counter, plus a cohort leaderboard.
- Your class: the same group and teacher every day, so people feel it when they skip.
- Career track (from B1): CV in German, the visa paperwork checklist, and job-board introductions.

**Pricing:**
- **Standard:** €149/month
- **Founding members (first 50):** €99/month, locked for life. Use this to get the first members fast.
- **Business (agencies/employers):** €129/seat/month, minimum 10 seats

---

## Who buys it

1. **People abroad who want to work in Germany.** Strongest in North Africa, the Middle East, India, the Philippines, Latin America, the Balkans and Eastern Europe. Nurses and care workers come first because their demand is the most urgent.
2. **People already in Germany** who need B1/B2 for a better job or for citizenship.
3. **Recruitment agencies and care employers** who bring in foreign staff. This is the fastest money: one contract for 30 seats is about €3,900/month.

> Note: €149 is a lot in many of these countries. That's why the program sells the **job outcome**, not "German lessons." It also offers a payment plan, and the B2B channel lets employers pay. Some countries also have public or employer funding for language training. Check it per market.

## 8-month plan

| Month | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **0–1** | Talk to 30 potential customers (nurse/care-worker Facebook groups, Telegram, WhatsApp communities). Put up the landing page and waitlist. Recruit 2 freelance teachers. | 100 people on the waitlist, 20 founding members paid |

| **7–8** | Sign more agency contracts, add customer support in the members' own languages (e.g. Arabic, English), automate onboarding. | 340 active members, about €70k cumulative profit |

## Main risks and what to do about them

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Too expensive for the target countries | Payment plans, agency/employer-paid seats, and a lower price in specific regions if the data supports it |
| People drop out after a few weeks | Fixed class groups, visible progress, weekly mock exams and an outcome guarantee: "Pass B1 or get extra months free" |
| Teacher quality or availability | Pay well (€25+/h), use a standard lesson plan and keep a waiting list of teachers |
| Competition (Goethe-Institut, Babbel, local schools) | You're the only one combining daily live classes, AI speaking practice and job placement, at a monthly price |
| Legal and payment setup | Register a business entity, collect VAT on EU sales, use Stripe or Paddle, and get a short terms-of-service review from an accountant |

## First 7 days

1. Pick one starting group: **nurses and care workers** who want to move to Germany.
2. Join 10 online communities where they gather and talk to 30 of them. Ask what they study, what they pay and what stops them.
3. Put the landing page (`index.html` in this repo) online with a waitlist form.
4. Find 2 German teachers (Preply, iTalki or local) willing to teach a 15-person group 5 days a week.
5. Offer founding membership at €99/month. **The goal is 20 people who actually pay before you build anything big.**

If you can't get 20 founding members in the first 4–6 weeks, change the target group or the offer before you spend money on building the app.

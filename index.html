Questions

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Most members start at A1. You'll be placed in a class at your level.

How long until I reach B1?

With 3–4 hours a day, most motivated learners can reach B1 in about 6–9 months. It depends on your starting point and how consistently you study.

Do you guarantee a job?

No one can honestly guarantee a job. We guarantee the training: if you follow the plan and don't pass B1, you get extra months free. From B1, we help with your CV, interviews and employer introductions.

What do I need?

A phone or laptop, headphones with a microphone and a stable internet connection.
